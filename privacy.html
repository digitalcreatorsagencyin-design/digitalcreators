Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Digital Creators (“we”, “us”) runs digitalcreatorsagency.in. This page explains what we collect when you contact us and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Details you enter in the enquiry form: your name, business name, mobile or WhatsApp number, email (optional), the service you want, your budget range (optional) and your message.
  • Anything you send us on WhatsApp, by phone, by email or on Instagram and Facebook.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and prepare a quotation.
  • To deliver the services you ask us to provide.

We do not sell your information. We do not send marketing messages to people who have not asked to hear from us.

Who can see it

Your enquiry is passed to us through the tools we use to receive messages, such as email, WhatsApp and our website hosting provider. We share information with others only when the law requires it or when you ask us to.

Demo websites

The sample websites linked from our Our Work section are demonstrations. Their booking and order buttons open a WhatsApp chat with Digital Creators, so a message sent from a demo comes to us.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry details while we are discussing work with you and for as long as we need them for our records. Ask us to delete your details and we will.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information we hold about you. Message us on WhatsApp at +91 99870 56334 or email digitalcreatorsagency.in@gmail.com.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not set advertising cookies. If we add analytics or advertising pixels in future, we will update this page first.
