Questions before you start

Is my ad budget included in the price?

No. Advertising spend is paid by you directly to Meta or Google. Our price covers strategy, setup, management and reporting.

Do you guarantee leads or sales?

No, and be careful of anyone who does. We plan for the best enquiries your offer and budget can earn, and we tell you early if something is not working.

Who follows up with the leads?

Your team does. We set up instant WhatsApp and form capture so enquiries reach you quickly. Visits, calls and closing stay with you.

What do I need to get started?

Your offer, your price range, photos or creatives if you have them, and a WhatsApp number that will answer enquiries. We help with the rest.

How are prices decided?

Starting prices are listed above. The final quote depends on your business category, scope and campaign complexity, and you approve it before we begin.

Does lead generation suit every business?

No. We check each business first, and if ads are not the right fit yet, we will say so.
